# The Psychology Bridge

This is the foundational insight behind claude-discipline: **agents respond to the same structural interventions that make humans productive.**

Break big tasks into small ones -> works for humans (GTD), works for agents ([[../planning/decompose-complexity]])

Set clear checkpoints -> works for humans (Agile), works for agents ([[../verification/validate-before-claiming]])

Reduce activation energy -> works for humans (atomic habits), works for agents ([[../agent-discipline/complexity-fear]])

Verify incrementally -> works for humans (TDD), works for agents ([[../verification/real-tests-only]])

Clean as you go -> works for humans (5S), works for agents ([[../agent-discipline/entropy-maximization]])

## Why This Matters

It means we don't need to invent new AI management techniques. We can apply decades of human productivity research. The harness design ([[../agent-discipline/harness-design]]) is just good project management adapted for agents.
